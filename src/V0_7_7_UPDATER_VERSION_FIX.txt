Halliseum Control Center v0.7.7
UPDATER VERSION FIX

Root cause:
- v0.7.6 contained the new persona files, but the Control Center still reported AppVersion 0.7.3.
- The window title also still said v0.7.5.
- The update checker compares latest.json to AppVersion, so it kept offering v0.7.6 after installation.

Permanent fix:
- Halliseum now reads its installed version from app_version.txt.
- The updater writes app_version.txt after every successful update.
- The updater validates the installed version marker before relaunching.
- The window title is generated from the real installed version.
- Fresh installs now report v0.7.7 correctly.
