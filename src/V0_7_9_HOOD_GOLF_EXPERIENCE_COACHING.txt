COACHING LEVELS UPGRADE
Rookie now assumes the golfer is still learning fundamentals and may teach grip, stance, posture, alignment, ball position, balance, centered contact and simple ball-flight concepts when relevant.

Amateur assumes those basics are already understood and focuses more on repeatability, strike, start line, curvature, dispersion, distance control and cause/effect.

Pro / Advanced assumes strong fundamentals and golf knowledge. It normally skips beginner setup lessons unless evidence identifies a fundamental breakdown, and focuses on precise delivery, pattern quality, launch/spin relationships, sequencing, dispersion and optimization.

IMPORTANT
Coaching Level changes the assumed experience, teaching depth and expectations. It never changes measured facts or launch-monitor/data limitations.
